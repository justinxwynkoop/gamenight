Effects: SFX Kit by Wyzowl, https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit
Thinking music: "Beginning 6" by HoliznaCC0 (album Title Screens), via https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio, with a clock tick from SFX Kit mixed in.
Theme music: "Happy Dance" by HoliznaCC0 (album Happy Pop Electronic, CC0), via https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio, trimmed to loop.

jeopardy__*.m4a are these recordings, named for the app's sound cues:
open, buzz, correct, wrong, dd, fanfare as named; timeup = "timeout";
roundstart = "board" fanfare; think = thinking music.
shared__lobby.m4a is the "Happy Dance" theme: it loops on the main menu and in every game's lobby.

Added for every game moment (all from SFX Kit, CC0): pick (a clue is picked; the Buzzworthy "pick" sound),
board = "data-countup-medium" (the board fills in), lock = "mechanical-lock-light" (a Final wager or answer
locks in), bet = "money-register" (a Daily Double bet), join = "notification-message-in-clear" (a player
joins), applause = "celebration-applause-small" (the end of a round, and the winner). Levels raised to match.

Nov 22 sound check: final = "sting-bold-long" (Final Jeopardy now has its own sting instead of the Daily Double one),
reveal = "tone-ding-mid" (its own ding instead of the time-up sound). applause, bet, board and think were raised and
wrong lowered, so every effect plays at about the same loudness (-18 LUFS; the thinking music -20).

spynight__*.m4a are Spy Night's own sounds, original and CC0: synthesized by Tools/make_spynight_sounds.py (it also writes the
website's copies in public/builtin-sounds/spynight/). lobby is the music that loops while players join and think the tenser loop under the game itself; the others are
named for the cues they play on: roundstart (the roles are dealt), pick (a team is being picked), open (the vote begins),
correct (a team is approved), wrong (rejected), lock (the mission begins), ding (a mission succeeds), strike (it fails),
fanfare (the Resistance win), winner (the Spies win), join (a player joins).

feud__lobby.m4a, feud__ding.m4a and feud__faceoff.m4a are Family Feud's own sounds (its theme tune, a right answer, and the face-off
buzzer), original and CC0, synthesized by Tools/make_spynight_sounds.py (it also writes the website's copies in
public/builtin-sounds/feud/). They are not recordings of the show.

guysvsgals__*.m4a are Guys vs Gals' own sounds, all CC0, from other people (the website's copies are in
public/builtin-sounds/guysvsgals/, whose CREDITS.txt lists every file). Music by HoliznaCC0 via Alvana Audio
(https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio): lobby = "Love Love Love", think = "50 Over The Speed Limit" (under a
charade). Effects from SFX Kit by Wyzowl (https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit): open, lock, correct, wrong,
timeup, pick (the trophy or a pawn moves), dd (a challenge is thrown, or a wild card in the race), ding (a trophy is won, or
a pawn gets across), lock (also a pawn taken prisoner), strike (not used any more),
roundstart (the game begins), fanfare (the winners), applause, join.
