Thinking music: "Beginning 6" by HoliznaCC0 (album Title Screens), via https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio, with a clock tick from SFX Kit mixed in.
Theme music: "Happy Dance" by HoliznaCC0 (album Happy Pop Electronic, CC0), via https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio, trimmed to loop.

jeopardy__*.m4a are these recordings, named for the app's sound cues:
open, buzz, correct, wrong, dd, fanfare as named; timeup and reveal = "timeout"; final = "dd";
roundstart = "board" fanfare; think = thinking music.
shared__lobby.m4a is the "Happy Dance" theme: it loops on the main menu and in every game's lobby.

Added for every game moment (all from SFX Kit, CC0): pick (a clue is picked; the Buzzworthy "pick" sound),
board = "data-countup-medium" (the board fills in), lock = "mechanical-lock-light" (a Final wager or answer
locks in), bet = "money-register" (a Daily Double bet), join = "notification-message-in-clear" (a player
joins), applause = "celebration-applause-small" (the end of a round, and the winner). Levels raised to match.
