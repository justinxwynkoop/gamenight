All sounds here are CC0 (public domain dedication), free for any use. None are recordings of a TV show or a board game.

Music, both by HoliznaCC0 (CC0), via Alvana Audio (https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio):
lobby = "Love Love Love" (album Happy Pop Electronic), the first two minutes, faded to loop while players pick their sides
think = "50 Over The Speed Limit" (album Retro Wave), 38 seconds: the music under a Lightning Round

Effects: SFX Kit by Wyzowl (CC0), https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit
open       = whoosh-medium-medium         a question comes up
lock       = pop-large-in                 the team's answer is locked in
correct    = notification-success-fanfare a right answer
wrong      = notification-failure-wobble  a wrong answer
timeup     = time-timer                   out of time
pick       = move-spring                  a token hops along the track
dd         = cinematic-thunder-big        a Lightning Round begins
ding       = tone-ding-high               a right answer in a Lightning Round
strike     = notification-failure-knock   a wrong answer in a Lightning Round
roundstart = sting-playful-long           the coin toss, the game begins
fanfare    = celebration-fireworks        the winners
applause   = celebration-applause-big     after the winners
join       = pop-medium-bubble            someone joins

Levelled with ffmpeg loudnorm (effects about -18 LUFS, music -20) and converted to mono mp3. The Apple TV app has the
same files as guysvsgals__<name>.m4a in GameNight/Sounds/. The family's own files (added from the host page) replace them.
