Spy Night's sounds were made for this project: they are synthesized from plain waves by tv-app/Tools/make_spynight_sounds.py
(run it again to remake them). They are original and dedicated to the public domain (CC0), free for any use.

lobby = the music that loops while players join; think = the quieter, tenser music that loops under the game itself, from the deal to the end (the shared theme in ../shared/ is used in the other games' lobbies).
The rest are named for the sound cues the app already has: roundstart = the roles are dealt, pick = a leader starts picking
a team, open = the vote begins, correct = a team is approved, wrong = a team is rejected, lock = the mission begins,
ding = a mission succeeds, strike = a mission fails, fanfare = the Resistance win, winner = the Spies win, join = a player joins.
The Apple TV app has the same files as spynight__<name>.m4a in GameNight/Sounds/.
