Family Feud's own sounds were made for this project: synthesized from plain waves by tv-app/Tools/make_spynight_sounds.py
(run `python3 tv-app/Tools/make_spynight_sounds.py feud` to remake them). They are original and dedicated to the public
domain (CC0), free for any use. They are not recordings of the TV show.

lobby  = Family Feud's theme tune: it loops while players join (the shared theme in ../shared/ plays in the other games' lobbies)
ding   = a right answer on the survey board
faceoff = the face-off buzzer
The Apple TV app has the same files as feud__<name>.m4a in GameNight/Sounds/. The family's own files (added from the host
page or the Mac script) replace them.
